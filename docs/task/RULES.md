# HubMI.pl — Regulations of the Task

> English version of the official "Regulamin zadania HubMI.pl" (HackYeah 2026). The Polish original and the copyright-assignment contract template are in the source PDF. Submissions and the pitch must be in **Polish**.

## § 1. General provisions

1. These regulations (hereinafter referred to as the “Regulations”) set out the rules and conditions of the “HubMI.pl” Competition, organized as part of the HackYeah event.
2. The Organizer of the Competition within the meaning of Article 921 § 3 of the Civil Code (Journal of Laws 2017, item 459) is Proidea Sp. z o.o., with its registered office at ul. Zakopiańska 9, 30-418 Kraków, with the following identification numbers: NIP (Tax Identification Number) 6793088842 and REGON (Business Identification Number) 122769022, entered in the National Court Register under KRS number 0000448243. The Organizer can be contacted by e-mail at biuro@proidea.org.pl.
3. Województwo Małopolskie, serving as the Host Region of HackYeah 2026, is the initiator of the “HubMI.pl” Competition.
4. Details of the task will be presented at the start of the competition.
5. The rules of participation in the Hackathon are set out in the Hackathon rules published on the Organizer’s website. Participation in the Competition is only possible for Hackathon participants.
6. The Organizer makes a promise to pay prizes to the Laureates on the terms set out in the Regulations, subject to the conclusion of a proprietary copyright assignment agreement with the Organizer (PROIDEA sp. z o.o.).
7. The purpose of the Regulations is to accurately describe the rules of participation in the Competition and they do not constitute a contract offer or a contract template.

## § 2. Duration of the Competition

1. The Competition starts on 3 October 2026 at 11:00 and ends on 4 October 2026 at 11:00.
2. A detailed schedule of the Competition will be made available on the Organizer’s website www.hackyeah.pl

## § 3. Conditions for participation

1. A participant in the Competition (hereinafter: the “Participant”) may be a natural person – a participant of the Hackathon who, on the day the Competition starts, is at least 18 years old and has full legal capacity.
2. Before joining the Competition, the Competition Participant is obliged to read the Regulations.
3. Participation in the Competition is tantamount to reading the Regulations and accepting the provisions of the Regulations and their annexes by the Competition Participant, and to the Competition Participant’s undertaking to comply with the provisions of the Regulations.
4. Participants may take part individually or in teams of up to 6 people.
5. Participants are obliged to comply with the Regulations and the Hackathon rules.

## § 4. Course of the Competition

1. The content of the “HubMI.pl” task will be published on the HackYeah website, www.hackyeah.pl, at 11:00 on the day the Competition starts.
2. The Competition Participants’ review of the content of the competition task and the commencement of solving it will be possible after the ceremonial opening of the Competition by the Organizer’s representative.
3. Competition Participants have a maximum of 24 hours to solve the competition task and submit their solution to the HackTribe platform.
4. Participation in the Hackathon and the Competition is only possible in an on-site form at Tauron Arena in Kraków (31-571), ul. Stanisława Lema 7.
5. Województwo Małopolskie and the Organizer do not provide Competition Participants with any equipment or hardware, nor do they provide technical support services or hardware repair.
6. Competition Participants may take part in the Competition individually or within Teams of no more than 6 people.
7. The competition task is carried out in an open formula (there is no single correct solution), and the project solutions of the competition task submitted by individual Competition Participants or Teams are assessed by the Competition Jury composed of representatives of Województwo Małopolskie and representatives appointed by the Organizer.
8. In order to be considered in the settlement of the Competition, the project solutions of the competition task prepared by individual Competition Participants or Teams must be submitted to the Organizer no later than 11:00 on 4 October 2026 in the form specified by the Organizer on the dedicated website (https://hackyeah2026.hacktribe.co/) on which the content of the competition task will be presented. Project solutions of the competition task submitted after this deadline will not be taken into account and will not be assessed by the Competition Jury.
9. The task submitted for evaluation must include: project title, team ID, project description, maximum 10-slides PDF presentation, maximum 3 minute mp4. video presenting a project and could include: snapshots, code repository, demo links, graphic materials and other materials related to the project. The task solutions should be submitted to platform Hacktribe in Polish.
10. Województwo Małopolskie or the Organizer may exclude a Participant/Team in the event of a breach of the Regulations or of the law; if a criminal offence is suspected, the Organizer will notify the competent authorities.

## § 5. Assessment rules

1. Solutions are assessed by the Competition Jury composed mainly of representatives of Województwo Małopolskie. The composition of the Jury will be published on the Hackathon website no later than on the start day.
2. The Jury elects a chairperson from among its members; decisions are made by a simple majority of votes in the presence of all members; in the event of a tie, the chairperson has the casting vote.
3. Each submission is assessed on a scale of 1-10 under the criteria below; the final score is a weighted average:
   - Challenge fulfilment - 40% Assessment considers how well the key components work and the number of additional features delivered
   - Implementation potential - 20% Assessment of the solution’s readiness for implementation. Higher scores will be awarded to products suitable for practical use that demonstrate scalability, flexibility, optimisation, cost-effectiveness and ease of maintenance,
   - Prototype accessibility and intuitiveness - 20% Assessment of whether the tool’s interface is clear and easy to use for all target user groups, regardless of age or digital literacy. Particular attention will be paid to whether the tool has been designed with WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards in mind to avoid creating barriers to use,
   - Bonus criteria - 20%, comprising:
     - Interface appeal, creativity and quality - 10% This criterion rewards innovative, unconventional approaches to the challenge and the visual appeal of the submitted UX/UI mock-ups,
     - Quality of the submitted materials and MVP - 10% Assessment considers how clearly the concept is communicated and the quality of the materials submitted for evaluation.
4. To become a Laureate, a submission must obtain at least 50% of the maximum possible score.
5. The project presentation before the Jury shall be conducted in Polish.

## § 6. Settlement and prizes

1. The results of the Competition will be announced on 4 October 2026 during the Hackathon closing ceremony (approx. 17:45) and on the event’s official messaging platform.
2. A maximum of three Winners will be selected (individual Participants or Teams) will be selected, taking places 1–3. The order is determined by the score under § 5; in the event of a tie – the chairperson’s vote decides.
3. Prizes:
   - 1st place - PLN 6 000,
   - 2nd place - PLN 5 000,
   - 3rd place - PLN 4 000.
4. The amounts of the prizes are reduced by due taxes, contributions and fees in accordance with the law.
5. In the case of awarding a Team, the prize is divided proportionally to the number of members and paid by the Organizer (PROIDEA sp. z o.o.) to each member to the indicated bank account.
6. The issuance of the prize is conditional upon the cumulative fulfilment by the Laureate/Laureates of:
   - **a)** conclusion with the Organizer (PROIDEA sp. z o.o.) of a proprietary copyright assignment agreement for the awarded works/solutions (the template/essential provisions will be provided by the Organizer);
   - **b)** provision of the data necessary for the Organizer to perform tax and settlement obligations.
7. The prizes will be paid by the Organizer to the Laureates’ bank accounts within 60 days from the conclusion of the agreement referred to in para. 6(a), unless the agreement provides otherwise.

1. Województwo Małopolskie and the Organizer are not liable for the inability to transfer the prize where the Competition Participant provides incorrect, incomplete or untrue personal data, contact details or other information preventing the transfer of the prize.
